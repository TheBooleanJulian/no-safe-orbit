| `artifacts/*.tres` (11 files) | NASA/JPL Horizons On-Line Ephemeris System (<https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/>), state vectors at 2026-10-19 12:00:00 UT, ICRF axes, queried 2026-10-20 through the Horizons API. Target (id) and the trajectory source Horizons reported: ISS (−125544) `iss`, Hubble (−48) `hst` — both from tracking elements (TLEs), about Earth; LRO (−85) `LRO_merged`, about the Moon; Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter (−74) `MRO_MERGED` and 2001 Mars Odyssey (−53) `Odyssey_merged`, about Mars; about the Sun: Tesla Roadster (−143205) `tesla_s11`, New Horizons (−98) `NH_merged`, Voyager 1 (−31) `Voyager_1_ST+refit2022_m`, Voyager 2 (−32) `Voyager_2_ST+refit2022_m`, Pioneer 10 (−23) `pioneer_10_merged`, Pioneer 11 (−24) `pioneer_11_merged`. MAVEN has no ephemeris after 2026-03-01 and is left out; JWST (at the Sun–Earth L2 point) needs a rotating frame the game does not have and is left out. Records (`lore`) are written for the game from public mission facts. | NASA/JPL data, free to use; credit JPL Horizons. |
